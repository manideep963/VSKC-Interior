About VSKC Interiors

Hyderabad's trusted turnkey interior design firm crafting dream spaces through German technology, transparent execution, and passionate craftsmanship.

Our Story

Redefining How Hyderabad Furnishes Homes

Founded with a bold vision to eliminate the unpredictability, delayed timelines, and inconsistent workmanship that often troubled the interior industry, VSKC Interiors has grown to become one of Hyderabad's most reputable turnkey residential interior design studios.

Headquartered in Kukatpally, our state-of-the-art Experience Center allows homeowners to touch and feel authentic materials, discover modular hardware innovations, and collaborate directly with experienced interior architects. With over 500+ successful handovers across Kukatpally, Gachibowli, HITEC City, Miyapur, and Tellapur, we pride ourselves on building spaces where memories flourish.
